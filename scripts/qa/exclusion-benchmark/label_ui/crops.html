Rules and keys

Count a problem only when it is substantial: roughly 20% or more of that layer in the crop, or 5% or more of the crop area. Lying wood is never deadwood. Hover a question for its full wording.

Work top to bottom on the highlighted row: y yes, n no, ? or u don't know; it moves down and after the last row goes to the next crop. ↑/↓ or 1–8 pick a row. Space nothing wrong, next. Enter remaining = no, next. c can't tell. Tab next open crop, ←/→ previous/next. Wheel and drag zoom, double-click resets.
