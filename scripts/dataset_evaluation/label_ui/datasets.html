Rules and keys

Keep: usable as it is. Keep, fix: usable, but the COG display, the thumbnail or the ortho needs fixing or reprocessing. Exclude: unusable as a whole for mapping dead trees and forest cover; pick every reason that applies. Unsure: you cannot tell from these images.

Season, georeferencing and duplicates have their own checks, but tag them as reasons when they are why you would exclude.
